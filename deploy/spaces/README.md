# DQ-Stack

Store operations and demand forecasting for Indian retail chains: every night it
forecasts every product in every store, sets each stock target from what it costs to
run out or be left over, and has the morning's orders ready to approve.

**Open the app here: [{{DIRECT_URL}}]({{DIRECT_URL}})**

The app opens at its own address, not inside this page: it refuses to be shown inside
another site, so that no other page can trick someone into clicking in it.

- `/app/` is the application. With demo logins on, the sign-in page lists the demo team:
  one click signs in as any of them.
- `/platform` is the operator's portal.

Source: [github.com/Praneet-R-S/dqstack](https://github.com/Praneet-R-S/dqstack).
