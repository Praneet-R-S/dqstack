The pipeline

Seven stages, each with a single responsibility and a typed interface. Stages three through seven all speak in predictive distributions — nothing downstream ever sees a point forecast, and that single interface contract is what allows the decision layer, the calibration layer and the economic evaluation to exist at all. Every number below came from the run that produced the forecast in the workspace.

Evidence

Three metric tiers — point accuracy, distributional quality, realised economics — and the two comparisons between tiers that the argument rests on: whether each stage adds value, and whether the most accurate configuration is the most profitable one.

Candidate leaderboard

Ranked by WSPL, the weighted scaled pinball loss, because it scores the whole predictive distribution rather than only its centre. WRMSSE is shown alongside for comparability with the M5 literature.

Forecast Value Added, stage by stage

A stage with negative Δ vs previous is destroying value at the margin even when its FVA against the naive anchor is still positive. Only the ladder shows that, which is why it is reported as a ladder. Every stage in this pipeline has an ablation switch for exactly this reason.

Calibration

Reliability diagram. The dotted diagonal is perfect calibration; the red dashed line is empirical coverage before conformal adjustment and the blue line after. Distance from the diagonal is the error the next stage would otherwise inherit when it reads a quantile off this distribution to set inventory.

Where the value lands, by planning class

The classes are not interchangeable and this is the table that proves it. A single global model applied to every series would be scored by the average of this column and would hide exactly the sparse-series behaviour that Stage 1 exists to route around.

Ensemble weights the meta-learner chose

Read across a row. Nobody wrote these rules — the meta-learner derived them from the Stage-1 series features, and they land where the forecasting literature says they should: the Croston family and temporal aggregation earn their weight on intermittent and lumpy series, gradient boosting on smooth ones. The seasonal-naive anchor is given almost nothing, which is the correct treatment of a benchmark.

Accuracy is not economics

Economics by configuration

Model promotion in this system is gated on realised ledger margin, not WRMSSE. The rows above are why. Promote on accuracy and the implementation contradicts the thesis.

Decisions

The link from forecast to money. The target service quantile is the critical fractile derived from cost asymmetry — underage over underage plus overage — which replaces the industry habit of applying a blanket 95% service level to everything.

The critical fractile in action

The eight lowest and eight highest target quantiles in the assortment. A low-margin, high-holding-cost, short-shelf-life item is planned far below 95%; a high-margin durable is planned above it. The formula says so precisely, and a blanket policy cannot express it.

Simulator ledger totals

Temporal reconciliation makes the daily, weekly and 28-day views arithmetically identical. "The weekly plan disagrees with the quarterly plan" becomes structurally impossible rather than a recurring meeting.
The conservation residual is the maximum discrepancy across all series between units in (opening plus received) and units out (sold plus wasted plus on hand). It is asserted as a unit test, not merely reported: a simulator that quietly loses units produces a ledger that looks reasonable and reports the wrong margin.
