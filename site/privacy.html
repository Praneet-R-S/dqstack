Who we are

DQ-Stack is a store operations app with demand forecasting built in. It is provided by [Company legal name], called "we" in this policy. You can reach us at [contact email].

This policy covers the DQ-Stack app and this website.

What DQ-Stack stores

When your company uses DQ-Stack, it stores the records the app needs to run your stores:

  • Company: your company's details.
  • Stores: the stores you add.
  • People: the people your company adds, the role each one has, the stores each one can see, and what they need to sign in. Passwords are stored only as hashes, never as the password itself.
  • Products: your product list.
  • Stock movements: every change to stock and its reason: a sale, a delivery, a count, wastage or a transfer.
  • Sales: the bills and daily sales your stores record or upload.
  • Orders: the draft orders the app suggests and the orders your team sends to suppliers.
  • Activity log: a record of the actions taken in the app. Entries can be added but not edited.

Why it is stored

These records are what DQ-Stack works from. They are used to:

  • keep an exact record of the stock in each of your stores;
  • forecast what will sell, with a range, a stock target and the reason behind each forecast;
  • suggest orders for your team to review, approve and send;
  • show each person only the stores they work in;
  • make sure no work is lost silently, and that every action can be traced.

Each company's data is kept apart

Every query is filtered by company on the server, so each company sees only its own records. Within a company, each person sees only their stores, and roles are checked on the server rather than only in the screen.

How it is protected

  • Passwords are stored as hashes.
  • Repeated failed sign in attempts lock the account.
  • What each person can see and do is checked on the server.
  • The activity log can only be added to, so the record of who did what stays intact.

Cookies

DQ-Stack uses one cookie: an essential session cookie, set when you sign in to the app, that keeps you signed in. It is not used for advertising or analytics. This website sets no cookies of its own.

Because the only cookie is strictly needed to sign in, there is no cookie consent banner. There are no optional cookies to accept or refuse.

Fonts from Google

The typefaces used by this website and by DQ-Stack are served by Google Fonts. To show them, your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, and like any web request, that request includes your IP address. Google describes what it collects in its Google Fonts privacy notes.

Export and deletion

If your company stops using DQ-Stack, we export all of your records on request, then delete them after a period we confirm with you.

To ask for an export, write to [contact email].

India's data protection law

Readiness for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is a goal we are working towards. We do not yet claim to meet it.
