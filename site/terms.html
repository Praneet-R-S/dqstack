About these terms

These terms cover your use of DQ-Stack, a store operations app with demand forecasting built in, and of this website. DQ-Stack is provided by [Company legal name], called "we" in these terms. "You" means the company that uses DQ-Stack and the people it adds.

Accounts and roles

Each person signs in with their own account. There are seven roles: Owner, Admin, Store manager, Purchaser, Stock clerk, Cashier and Viewer. Each person sees only their stores, and roles are checked on the server.

Keep your sign in details private. Repeated failed sign in attempts lock the account.

Your records

Your company's records are kept apart from every other company's: every query is filtered by company on the server. The privacy policy sets out what is stored and why.

Forecasts and orders

Every forecast comes with its range, its stock target and the reason behind it. A forecast is an estimate of future sales, not a promise of them.

Your first forecast comes with a backtest on your own last 8 weeks. That backtest is the accuracy figure to rely on; we do not promise any other accuracy figure.

Suggested orders are drafts. A person in your company approves each supplier's draft order before it is sent, and your company decides what it orders.

Stock and offline work

Stock is never typed over. Every change is a movement with a reason, and stock changes only when something is final: a paid bill, a received delivery or an approved count. The forecast learns from this record, so it is only as good as the entries your team makes.

Work saves as it happens and keeps working offline. An entry cannot be counted twice, and every action is logged.

Using DQ-Stack fairly

Use DQ-Stack for your own company's stores. Do not try to reach another company's records, get around a role, or disrupt the service for other users.

Bills and taxes

We do not claim that bills from the optional Checkout screen meet GST invoicing rules. Confirm your invoicing needs with your chartered accountant.
