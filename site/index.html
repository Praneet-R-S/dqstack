One street. Every shop on it closes with the same question.

Behind every shutter, someone decides what to order for tomorrow.

Most nights, it is a guess.

Two ways to get it wrong.

Too little. The shelf is empty when the customer comes, and the sale walks out of the door.

Too much. The stock sits until it nears expiry, then leaves at half price.

DQ-Stack turns every label into one number.

Every sale, delivery, count and festival goes into tomorrow's forecast. Each morning the order is waiting, with the range it came from and the reason for it.

How the number is made.

The same loop, every night, for every product in every store.

  1. During the day

    Sales, deliveries and counts come in.

    From the DQ-Stack till, your own billing software, Tally or a spreadsheet. Stock is never typed over; every movement is written down.

  2. Overnight

    The forecast runs.

    Each product gets a range for the days ahead, learned from its own sales, the season and the festivals in your state, and tested on weeks it has not seen.

  3. By morning

    Orders are suggested, with reasons.

    The target covers the days until the next delivery, the order rounds to the supplier's case, and every line says why.

  4. At opening

    You decide the few that need you.

    Today shows only what needs a person: an order to approve, a count that does not add up, a product about to run out.

Questions before you start

Do we need barcode scanners or any new hardware?

No. A browser and a spreadsheet are enough to start. Any USB or Bluetooth scanner that types like a keyboard works when you want one, and a phone camera can scan for counts and deliveries.

We already have billing software. Do we have to replace it?

No. Keep it, and upload the day's sales file or let it send each sale to DQ-Stack as it happens. Tally and Zoho Inventory connect directly. The DQ-Stack till is optional.

How much sales history do we need?

Two weeks of daily sales give a first estimate, clearly labelled, and eight weeks give full forecasting. Twelve months lets the forecast learn your own festivals, and two to three years is best.

How do we know the forecast is any good for our stores?

Your first forecast is tested on your own most recent weeks of sales, which it has not seen, and you see how close it came before you rely on it. After that it is scored every night against what actually sold.

What happens when the internet goes down?

The till keeps billing. Bills are kept on the device in order and sent when the connection returns, and a bill sent twice is counted once. Counting and receiving on a phone keep working too.

Who can see our data?

Each person sees only the stores they are assigned to, and only what their role allows. Every company's data is kept apart. DQ-Stack support can see yours only when an Owner grants access for a set time, and every look is recorded.

Does it work with Indian festivals and formats?

Yes. Amounts are in rupees with lakh and crore, times are in IST, and national and state festivals come filled in for each store's state. You add local events and closures. Store staff can use the app in Hindi.

Can we leave and take our data with us?

Yes. You can export every record as CSV and Excel, and your data is deleted after a confirmed period.

Can the DQ-Stack till issue our GST invoices?

It prints receipts with the GST shown. Before you use it as your legal billing system, have your chartered accountant confirm the invoice format. Until then it works as a stock register beside your current billing.
