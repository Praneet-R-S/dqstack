Stock and ordering for Indian retailers

Order what will sell,
from stock you can trust

One product, two jobs that feed each other

The inventory checker keeps stock exact. The forecaster turns that record into what to order. Each one feeds the other.

The inventory checker

Keeps an exact record of what is on the shelf in every store: each sale, delivery, count, wastage and transfer.

The forecaster

Says what will sell and how much to order, with a range, a stock target and the reason behind every number.

Why exact stock makes a better forecast

When a shelf is empty, sales read zero. A forecaster that cannot see the shelf takes those days as days nobody wanted the product, and learns to order less.

DQ-Stack knows which days a product was out of stock, so it does not have to guess.

Know what is on the shelf. Know what will sell. Order the difference.

Every number can be traced back

Stock, forecasts and orders all show how they were reached, so your team can check them instead of taking them on trust.

Stock is never typed over

Every change is a movement with a reason: a sale, a delivery, a count, wastage or a transfer. Stock on hand is the sum of those movements, so any number can be followed line by line.

And stock changes only when something is final: a paid bill, a received delivery, an approved count.

How it works

Set up once, check the forecast against your own sales, then run the same cycle every day.

  1. 1

    Set up with a guided checklist

    Add your stores and your people, then upload your products and your sales history from a spreadsheet.

  2. 2

    Check the backtest on your own sales

    Your first forecast comes with a backtest on your own last 8 weeks. That is the accuracy figure to trust, because it comes from your stores.

    See the planner demo
  3. 3

    Run the daily cycle

    Close the day, let the forecast refresh overnight, then review and order in the morning.

The daily cycle

  1. Evening Close the day The store manager closes the day.
  2. Overnight The forecast refreshes Ready for the morning review.
  3. Morning Review the Today screen About 20 flagged products. Approve, change with a reason, or skip.
  4. After review Approve draft orders The manager approves each supplier's draft order.
  5. Then Send the orders The purchaser sends them by email, WhatsApp or PDF.

Every week the models retrain. A new model replaces the current one only if it would have earned more profit in the backtest.

Questions before you start

Plain answers about setup, accuracy and your data.

Do I need a barcode scanner or new billing software?

No. A browser and a spreadsheet upload are enough to start. Barcode scanners and links to your existing billing software are optional upgrades you can add store by store.

How accurate will the forecast be for my stores?

Your first forecast comes with a backtest on your own last 8 weeks of sales. That is the accuracy figure to trust, because it is measured on your stores rather than quoted from someone else's.

Can someone simply change a stock number?

No. Stock is never typed over. Every change is a movement with a reason, such as a sale, a delivery, a count, wastage or a transfer, and stock changes only when something is final: a paid bill, a received delivery or an approved count.

How is the suggested order worked out?

It is the stock target, minus stock on hand, minus what is already on order, rounded up to the case pack and raised to the supplier minimum. The store manager approves each supplier's draft order before the purchaser sends it.

What happens when the internet goes down?

Work saves as it happens and keeps working offline. Nothing can be counted twice, and every action is logged. If you use the optional Checkout screen, it can keep billing offline too.

Can one store use a scanner while another uploads a file?

Yes. Each store runs standalone, with a daily sales file or typed totals, or connected, with a scanner or your existing billing software. A company can run both at once.

Who in my team sees what?

There are seven roles: Owner, Admin, Store manager, Purchaser, Stock clerk, Cashier and Viewer. Each person sees only their stores, and roles are checked on the server.

Does it handle Indian formats and festivals?

Yes. Amounts are in rupees with lakh and crore, times are in IST, and festival calendars are set by state.

How do orders reach suppliers?

Once a draft order is approved, the purchaser sends it by email, WhatsApp or PDF.

How is our data kept apart from other companies?

Every query is filtered by company on the server. Passwords are stored as hashes, accounts lock after repeated failed sign in attempts, and an activity log that can only be added to records every action.

What happens to our records if we stop using DQ-Stack?

We export all of your records on request, then delete them after a period we confirm with you.
